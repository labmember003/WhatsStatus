Saw a funny video or a lovely photo in someone's status? Save it before it disappears after 24 hours.

Status Saver keeps the photos and videos you have watched in your chat app's status feed, so you can save them, share them or repost them to your own status.

NEW: Group Status
Group Status updates are statuses shared only with the members of one group. Status Saver picks them up too. Open a group's status in your chat app, come back to Status Saver, and it is there, ready to save.

What you can do
• Save status photos and videos to your phone's gallery
• Save Group Status updates, the same way as any other status
• Repost a status to your own status, or share it to any app
• Works with personal and Business accounts; switch with one tap
• Watch videos and view photos full-screen before you save them
• Find everything you saved in the Saved tab, even offline
• Saves the exact file from your phone, with no extra compression

How to use it
1. Open your chat app and watch the status or Group Status you want to keep.
2. Open Status Saver. The status shows up in the Available tab.
3. Tap it, then tap Save. It goes to your gallery and the Saved tab.

The first time, Status Saver asks you to pick the chat app's folder. A short guide shows you which folder to choose and which button to tap.

Privacy
• Everything stays on your phone. Status Saver reads only the status folder you allow, and only to show and save statuses.
• No sign-up, no account, and we don't read your chats or contacts.
• The app shows a small banner ad, and asks for your consent first where the law requires it.

Please respect other people's content
Save and share only statuses you have the right to use, and ask the owner before you repost their status.
